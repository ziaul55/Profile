Welcome

Biomedical Vision and Learning is a research group under Center for Machine Vision and Signal Analysis, University of Oulu, led by Dr. Md. Ziaul Hoque and Prof. Tapio Seppänen. We aim to develop machine learning and deep learning-driven AI-based solutions for objective diagnosis, prognosis, and biomarker discovery.

BioVision research team is dedicated to developing automated, and objective mechanisms to reduce interobserver and intraobserver variability in cancer diagnosis, utilizing artificial intelligence as an assistive tool for pathologists or doctors.

The team focuses on creating novel algorithms and methodologies to identify clinically relevant morphological phenotypes and biomarkers associated with therapeutic responses to specific agents. The team also focuses on developing multimodal fusion algorithms that integrate information from multiple imaging modalities, familial and patient histories, and multi-omics data to enable more precise diagnostic, prognostic, and therapeutic determinations.

We share code and data where we can, and we welcome collaboration with clinical and biomedical partners.

Research

Medical image analysis

Segmentation, detection and quantification in histopathology, radiology and microscopy images.

Learning with limited labels

Self-supervised, semi-supervised and few-shot methods that make the most of a small set of expert annotations.

Trustworthy and 3D biomedical vision

Reliable predictions across sites and devices, and models that work on volumes, video and multimodal data.
